# Module 9 — Market Cycles & Narratives

**Est. length:** 3 days (6 hours).
**Prerequisite:** Modules 4–5, 8. **Feeds into:** Module 10+ (meme-coin discovery lives entirely inside "what narrative/cycle phase are we in right now").

---

## 1. What You Need to Learn

Bull/bear markets, accumulation/distribution, capital rotation, altcoin seasons, how narratives form, and sector rotation — i.e., the macro backdrop that determines whether meme coins (and altcoins generally) are even a favorable environment to be trading right now.

## 2. Why It Matters

Meme-coin trading conditions are not constant. In a genuine "alt season"/risk-on narrative environment, capital rotates aggressively into small, speculative assets; in a BTC-dominant, risk-off environment, the same meme coin that "would have" pumped instead dies with no volume. Reading the cycle correctly is a precondition for Module 10's discovery work making any sense.

## 3. Foundational Concepts

- **Bull market:** a sustained uptrend across most of the market, typically with rising participation (breadth), rising on-chain activity, and eventually excessive speculation/leverage.
- **Bear market:** a sustained downtrend, typically with falling participation, capitulation events, and eventually quiet, low-volume accumulation.
- **Accumulation and distribution:** informal Wyckoff-style framing — accumulation is quiet, range-bound buying by patient/larger participants after a decline (low volatility, low retail interest); distribution is the mirror image at the top of a rally (choppy, high-volume, retail euphoria) before markdown begins. These are **read from price/volume/on-chain behavior after the fact more easily than predicted in real time** — treat cycle-phase calls as probabilistic reads, not certainties.

## 4. Beginner Concepts

- **Capital rotation, the basic sequence:** in a typical risk-on expansion, capital tends to flow stablecoins → BTC → ETH/large-caps → mid-cap alts → small-caps/meme coins, roughly in that order of risk appetite; it reverses (flows back toward BTC/stablecoins) as risk appetite falls. This is a **general tendency observed across past cycles, not a fixed law** — verify current conditions rather than assuming the sequence is playing out.
- **Altcoin season:** a period where altcoins broadly outperform BTC — identified using BTC dominance (falling), the ETH/BTC ratio (rising), and market breadth (Module 8) together, not any single indicator alone.
- **Narrative:** a story the market is currently trading on (e.g., "AI coins," "RWA tokenization," "a specific meme-coin trend") that concentrates capital and attention into a sector, independent of — sometimes far ahead of — any underlying fundamental development.

## 5. Intermediate Concepts

- **Sector rotation:** within "alt season," capital doesn't move evenly — it rotates between sectors/narratives (DeFi, gaming, AI, memes) sequentially, often triggered by one breakout project or catalyst pulling attention (and capital) toward its whole sector.
- **How narratives form (mechanically):** a catalyst (a product launch, a viral moment, a headline) draws attention → early movers profit and post about it → broader social media amplification (Module 11) → new capital chases the narrative → the narrative peaks when a large majority of interested capital has already entered → the narrative fades as attention (and often price) moves to the next one. Recognizing which stage a narrative is in is more useful than recognizing that a narrative exists.
- **Social momentum vs. fundamental development:** a narrative can run entirely on attention with no underlying fundamental change (pure meme momentum) or can be genuinely tied to a real development (e.g., a real technical upgrade) — distinguishing the two is a core skill built fully in Modules 10–11, introduced here at the macro level.

## 6. Advanced Concepts

- **Reading "where we are" using multiple corroborating signals, not one:** BTC dominance direction + ETH/BTC ratio + market breadth + funding/OI regime (Module 8) + qualitative narrative strength together, not any single one, form your best-available (still uncertain) read of the current cycle phase.
- **Why meme-coin viability is cycle-dependent:** meme coins are the highest-beta expression of "risk-on, narrative-driven, small-cap-favorable" conditions. The same discovery/vetting skill (Modules 10–15) produces very different real-world results depending on whether the broader cycle currently supports small-cap speculation — this is context you must check before every meme-coin session, not a one-time lesson.
- **Cycle-phase honesty:** no one, including this course, can tell you with certainty which cycle phase you're in right now in real time — only build a probabilistic, evidence-labeled read, and update it as new data arrives, exactly like every other judgment in this course.

## 7. Practical Exercises

- Using Module 4/5/8 data (BTC dominance trend, ETH/BTC ratio, funding/OI regime) plus a market-breadth check, write your current best-available cycle-phase read (accumulation/early bull/late bull/distribution/bear/early recovery), labeled Hypothesis, with your evidence listed.
- Identify one currently-active crypto narrative (any sector) and trace, as best you can from public information, roughly which stage it's in (early, peaking, fading) using the §5 narrative-lifecycle framework.

## 8. Drills

- **Cycle-signal drill:** given hypothetical BTC dominance, ETH/BTC ratio, and breadth readings, state the most likely (not certain) cycle phase and your confidence level.
- **Narrative-stage drill:** given a hypothetical narrative's social-attention and price trajectory, identify whether it looks early, peaking, or fading.

## 11. Assessments
