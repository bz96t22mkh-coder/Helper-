## 1. How This Course Is Different From Your ICT Course

You are not starting from zero as a trader. You are starting from zero **in crypto**. This course assumes fluency in: market structure, liquidity (buy-side/sell-side, draw on liquidity, sweeps), BOS/CHoCH/MSS, CISD, displacement, FVG/IFVG, order blocks, breaker/mitigation blocks, BPR, premium/discount, OTE, dealing ranges, HTF→LTF, sessions/killzones, Judas Swing, AMD, Power of 3, SMT, risk management, backtesting, bar replay, journaling, and prop-firm-style process discipline.

None of that is retaught here. Every time this course touches a concept you already know, it will say so explicitly and move straight to **"here's what's the same, here's what's different in crypto, here's how to test it"** rather than re-explaining the concept from scratch.

What this course *does* teach from zero: crypto itself — the ecosystem, the mechanics, the tools, BTC/ETH specialization, crypto-specific data (funding, OI, on-chain), narratives/cycles, and — as a full second specialization — meme-coin trading: discovery, X/Twitter research, tokenomics, on-chain due diligence, scam detection, and a complete, testable meme-coin trading framework.

## 2. The Learning Loop

```
LEARN → APPLY (chart/research/on-chain) → RECORD → REVIEW/TEST → ADAPT → RETEST
```

For BTC/ETH: this loop runs as **TEST YOUR EXISTING ICT MODEL, don't invent a new one** unless the data tells you to. For meme coins: this loop runs as **DISCOVER → VALIDATE → PLAN → EXECUTE (paper first) → JOURNAL → REVIEW**, since meme coins can't be backtested the way BTC/ETH can (see Module 16).

## 3. The Daily 2-Hour Engine

Every day gets a purpose-built 2-hour breakdown — never a copy-pasted template. The mix always sums to 120 minutes, but the ratio of Lesson : Practical : Research/Chart/On-chain : Assignment/Journal shifts with what stage you're in:

| Stage | Typical mix |
|---|---|
| Early (Modules 1–2: fundamentals, tools, setup) | Lesson-heavy: ~45 min lesson, ~45 min hands-on setup/practice, ~30 min review/quiz |
| Middle (Modules 3–11: mechanics, BTC/ETH, ICT application, on-chain, meme-coin research) | Research/chart-heavy: ~20 min lesson, ~50–60 min chart/research/on-chain work, ~30–40 min assignment/journal |
| Advanced (Modules 12–19: security, framework, backtesting, paper trading, refinement) | Practice-heavy: ~15 min lesson/review, ~70–80 min backtesting/replay/paper trading/due-diligence, ~25–30 min stats/journal review |

You will never open this course and have to guess what to do with your 2 hours. Every day in every module file has an exact table: segment, minutes, action.

## 4. Mastery Levels (used every module)

Each module opens with a **baseline check** (what you already know/can do) and closes with an **exit assessment** (a harder, less-scaffolded version). You're rated on demonstrated performance, not lesson completion:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Beginner | Can't yet produce the skill/analysis reliably, even with support |
| Developing | Produces it inconsistently, needs prompting |
| Competent | Produces it reliably under practice conditions |
| Advanced | Produces it reliably under real/live conditions, spontaneously |
| Highly Proficient | Applies it naturally across varied market conditions |
| Mastery | Could teach it, adapts it on the fly, catches its own edge cases |

You need **Competent** on a module's exit assessment to move to the next module. Some skills (e.g., on-chain literacy, X/Twitter vetting) keep maturing through use rather than needing Mastery before progressing — those are flagged per-module.

## 10. Honesty & Evidence Policy (binding for this entire course)

- No coin, setup, or narrative is ever presented as a guaranteed winner.
- ICT is never assumed to "just work" on crypto — Module 7 exists specifically to test that, with real stats, before you trust it.
- X/Twitter sentiment, whale wallets, and influencer calls are treated as **inputs to investigate**, never as **evidence of profitability** by themselves.
- Every claim is labeled: **Fact** (verifiable/on-chain/exchange data), **Hypothesis** (testable trading idea), **Narrative** (a story the market is currently trading on — may or may not be true), or **Speculation/Unverified** (can't currently be confirmed).
- Security guidance is followed strictly: you will never be asked for seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or 2FA codes.
