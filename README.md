# Self-Directed Courses

This repository holds performance-based, self-directed curricula built and coached by Claude — each in its own folder, with a master curriculum, a day-by-day training plan for every section/module, and a progress tracker.

A complete, 20-section, performance-based personal-development curriculum covering meta-learning, English/articulation, communication, social skills, confidence, charisma, storytelling, critical thinking, debating, productivity, problem-solving, decision-making, negotiation, networking, leadership, strategic thinking, financial literacy, and meditation/mindfulness — trained in 1-hour daily sessions, with a full day-by-day plan for every section.

- **Start here:** [`course/00-master-curriculum.md`](course/00-master-curriculum.md) — the full curriculum map, section order and rationale, priority tiers, estimated training length per section, the daily-session system, and the assessment/progression rules.
- **Progress tracking:** [`course/progress-tracker.md`](course/progress-tracker.md) — updated as sections are completed.

## Ultimate Crypto Trading & Meme-Coin Course

A completely separate, performance-based crypto curriculum — built for someone who has **already completed an ICT Futures Day-Trading Course** and is starting from zero in crypto, not in trading. It does not re-teach ICT; it teaches how much of it transfers to BTC/ETH, what's crypto-native on top (on-chain data, tokenomics, funding/OI, market cycles), and a full second specialization in meme-coin trading (discovery, X/Twitter research, security/scam detection, on-chain due diligence, and a structured, testable trading framework) — trained in 2-hour daily sessions.

- **Start here:** [`crypto-course/00-master-curriculum.md`](crypto-course/00-master-curriculum.md) — the full curriculum map, module order and rationale, time estimates (learning/practical/research/chart/on-chain/backtesting), the daily-session system, the Level 1–7 skill ladder, and the honesty/evidence policy.
- **Progress tracking:** [`crypto-course/progress-tracker.md`](crypto-course/progress-tracker.md) — daily log, weekly rollup, module log, updated as you go.
