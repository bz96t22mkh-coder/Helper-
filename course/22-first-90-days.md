# Module 22 — Your First 90 Days

A realistic, week-by-week roadmap from absolute beginner to an operating creator. Adjust timing to your actual availability — the sequence matters more than the exact calendar dates.

## 1. Concept

The first 90 days is where you move through Modules 1–17 in practice, not just in theory, while producing your first real batch of videos (the start of Module 21's 100-video arc). This module is the execution schedule that ties the whole course together into a calendar.

## 2. Weeks 1–2: Foundation and setup

- **Learn**: Modules 1–3 (how YouTube works, your brand, pillar strategy).
- **Practice**: Module 10 daily speaking program, Days 1–10. Module 12 filming setup test.
- **Film**: nothing public yet — test footage only, reviewing against Module 12's checklist.
- **Upload**: none yet.
- **Analyze**: n/a yet.
- **Improve**: finalize your brand one-pager (Module 2) and content pillar map (Module 3) as working drafts.

## 3. Weeks 3–4: First videos

- **Learn**: Modules 4–5 (gaming/reaction craft), 9 (scripting).
- **Practice**: Module 10 program, Days 11–20. Complete your Editor Brief template (Module 13) and establish that workflow with your editor.
- **Film**: your first 2–4 videos using the appropriate format templates (Module 9).
- **Upload**: publish video 1 and video 2, establishing your initial cadence (see Module 23).
- **Analyze**: start your Per-Video Analytics Log (Module 17) — data will be sparse, that's fine.
- **Improve**: run your first retention diagnostic (Module 11) once you have any data.

## 4. Weeks 5–6: Titles, thumbnails, and idea systems

- **Learn**: Modules 6–8 (ideas, titles, thumbnails).
- **Practice**: Module 10 program, Days 21–30 (complete the 30-day program).
- **Film**: videos 5–8, applying the Idea Scoring System (Module 6) and full Title Brainstorm process (Module 7) before each.
- **Upload**: continue your established cadence.
- **Analyze**: weekly analytics review (Module 17) becomes a standing habit starting now.
- **Improve**: apply your first retention-driven fix (Module 11) to a script/edit based on real data from weeks 3–4's videos.

## 5. Weeks 7–8: Shorts and cross-platform

- **Learn**: Modules 14–15 (Shorts, cross-platform).
- **Practice**: build your first Shorts repurposing pipeline (Module 14) from your existing long-form videos.
- **Film**: videos 9–12, plus original Shorts content between uploads.
- **Upload**: begin cross-posting to at least one short-form platform (TikTok or Reels) using Module 15's system.
- **Promote**: light-touch cross-platform CTAs per Module 15.
- **Analyze**: compare Shorts performance against long-form in your weekly review.

## 6. Weeks 9–10: Community and consistency

- **Learn**: Module 16 (community).
- **Practice**: reply consistently to comments; post your first community posts.
- **Film**: videos 13–16, incorporating any early recurring bits/series identified from Module 2's ongoing observation.
- **Upload**: maintain cadence — by now this should feel like an established rhythm.
- **Promote**: begin light Discord/X/Reddit engagement per Module 15 if not already active.
- **Analyze**: check returning-viewer percentage trend (Module 17) — an early community-health signal.

## 7. Weeks 11–12: Systemization and review

- **Learn**: Module 23 (weekly creator system) — formalize what you've been doing ad hoc for 10 weeks into an explicit repeatable workflow.
- **Practice**: run one full week using the exact Module 23 workflow stage by stage.
- **Film**: videos 17–20+.
- **Upload**: maintain cadence.
- **Analyze**: run your first full 90-day retrospective (template below).
- **Improve**: update your brand one-pager, content pillar map, and idea backlog with everything learned; begin drafting Module 24's personal playbook.

## 8. 90-day retrospective template

```
Videos published: ___
Best-performing video and why (per Module 17 diagnosis): ___
Worst-performing video and why: ___
Content pillar performing best so far: ___
On-camera skill change (compare earliest vs. most recent footage): ___
Community/engagement signals (comments, returning viewers): ___
What I'm changing for days 91+: ___
```

## 9. Checklist — end of 90 days

- [ ] 15–20+ videos published on a consistent cadence
- [ ] Module 10's 30-day speaking program completed
- [ ] Weekly analytics review is a standing habit
- [ ] At least one Shorts/cross-platform pipeline running
- [ ] Editor workflow (Module 13) running smoothly with minimal revision rounds
- [ ] Brand one-pager and content pillar map updated with real data
- [ ] 90-day retrospective completed

## 10. Common mistakes

- Trying to do everything (Shorts, cross-platform, community, brand deals) at full intensity from week 1 instead of following the sequence — overwhelm is the fastest route to burnout and inconsistency.
- Skipping the weekly analytics habit until "there's more data" — the habit matters more early, when it's cheap to build, than late when you're retrofitting it onto 50 unlogged videos.
- Abandoning the plan after a discouraging week 3 or 4 instead of trusting the phased approach (this mirrors Module 21's framing directly).

## 11. What beginners waste time on

- Perfecting the brand/setup in weeks 1–2 instead of moving to actual filming once "good enough" is reached — see Module 2's warning against over-indexing on visual identity before personality is even tested on camera.
- Treating this as a rigid, inflexible calendar instead of a sequence — if week 5 needs an extra week, take it; the order matters more than exact dates.

## 12. Homework

Adapt this 12-week structure to your actual real-world availability (a full-time-job schedule will stretch this differently than a full-time-creator schedule) and commit to the sequence.

## 13. Checkpoint

At the end of your 90 days, you should be able to fill out the full retrospective template with real, specific answers — not blanks. If several sections are empty, identify which phase you skipped and revisit it before treating the 90 days as complete.
