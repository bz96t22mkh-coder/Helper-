# Section 19 — Advanced Integration

**Length:** 9 days. **No new skills.** Real situations never arrive labeled by section — a hard conversation needs articulation, EQ, confidence, and negotiation all at once. This is where the 18 separate trainings become one thing.

---

### Day 1 — Baseline + First Combination

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 25 | Rate yourself 1–6 (Beginner→Mastery) on all 18 prior sections, as you currently feel — no re-testing yet. |
| 25 | In a real conversation today, tell a story that also requires you to state an opinion clearly and read the listener's reaction. |
| 10 | Which skills did you reach for without thinking, and which did you have to consciously recall? |

### Day 2 — Negotiation Under Emotional Pressure

*(Negotiation + EQ + Communication + Confidence)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Predict which sections you'll need. |
| 45 | Run a real or detailed roleplay negotiation where the other party is emotionally charged. Use EQ to read and regulate, communication to stay clear, negotiation structure throughout. |
| 10 | Tag which skills you actually used vs. predicted. |

### Day 3 — Leading Through Disagreement

*(Leadership + Debating + Communication + EQ)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Predict which sections you'll need. |
| 45 | Take a real or realistic scenario where you must make and defend a decision two people disagree with. Use full-argument structure, ownership language, and EQ to keep it non-adversarial. |
| 10 | Tag skills used. |

### Day 4 — High-Stakes Introduction

*(Articulation + Social Skills + Confidence + Storytelling)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Predict which sections you'll need. |
| 45 | Meet or re-engage a real professional contact: sharp self-introduction, a spontaneous relevant story, confident presence, genuine rapport-building. |
| 10 | Tag skills used. |

### Day 5 — Checkpoint

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Recall Days 1–4's skill tags. |
| 35 | A new combined real-world task drawing on 4+ prior sections of your choice, based on a real situation this week. |
| 20 | Which skill cluster is currently strongest, and which needs the most work? |

### Day 6 — Strategic Financial Decision

*(Critical Thinking + Decision-Making + Strategic Thinking + Financial Literacy)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Predict which sections you'll need. |
| 45 | Take a real financial or resource-allocation decision. Reason through it with evidence rigor, expected value, second-order effects, and your actual numbers. |
| 10 | Tag skills used. |

*(Debating + Negotiation + Confidence + EQ)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|

### Day 8 — Teach and Lead

*(Meta-Learning + Storytelling + Articulation + Leadership)*

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Predict which sections you'll need. |
| 45 | Teach a real concept Feynman-style to someone, using story where it helps, with clear direction if it's a group setting. |
| 10 | Tag skills used. |

### Day 9 — Capstone + Decay Check

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 30 | Design and run your own real, high-stakes combined challenge, your choice, drawing on 5+ prior sections. |
| 20 | Review all 18 sections. Flag any skill that's visibly decayed since its section ended. |
| 10 | Which decayed skills need a refresher before the final section? |

**Mastery bar:** you identify and blend 3+ relevant skills in a real complex situation without consciously "switching modes." If not yet, run 2–4 more combined challenges targeting your weakest cluster.
