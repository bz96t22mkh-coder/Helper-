# Section 14 — Networking

**Length:** 9 days. **Goal:** a small number of real, mutually valuable relationships — not a pile of contacts who vaguely know your name.

---

### Day 1 — Introducing Yourself

A short, clear statement of who you are and what you care about — no over-explaining, no under-selling. Say it enough times that it stops sounding rehearsed.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 15 | Baseline: describe your current networking habits. How often do you meet new contacts, and follow up? |
| 20 | Write and practice a 15-second and a 30-second self-introduction. |
| 15 | What do you usually lead with, and does it represent what you want to be known for? |
| 10 | Reflection. |

### Day 2 — Starting a Professional Conversation

Same skill as any social opener, aimed at a professional context — a shared situation or a genuine question about their work.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Practice your self-introduction once more. |
| 30 | Prepare 3 situational openers for a professional context you'll actually encounter. Use one for real if possible. |
| 15 | How is a professional opener different from a purely social one? |
| 10 | Reflection. |

### Day 3 — Give Before You Ask

Offering something useful — an introduction, a resource, help — before you need anything builds a relationship that can actually hold a future ask.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Warm-up. |
| 25 | For 2 real people you know, identify one genuine piece of value you could offer with no ask attached. |
| 15 | Send one of them today. |
| 15 | Did it feel different offering something with no immediate ask? |

### Day 4 — The Follow-Up Is Where It Actually Happens

The first conversation is just an opener. A specific message referencing something real from that conversation is what turns it into an actual relationship.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Warm-up. |
| 25 | Write a specific, non-generic follow-up for a real past contact, referencing an actual detail from your conversation. |
| 15 | Send it. |
| 15 | How does a specific follow-up differ from a generic "great meeting you"? |

### Day 5 — Checkpoint

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Practice self-introduction. |
| 30 | Introduce yourself to someone new (or re-engage a contact) with a value-first angle, and send a specific follow-up. |
| 15 | Rep on weakest element. |
| 10 | Progress vs. Day 1. |

### Day 6 — Staying in Touch Without an Agenda

A genuine, no-ask check-in every so often keeps a relationship warm. Reaching out only when you need something is what makes it feel transactional.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Warm-up. |
| 25 | Send one genuine, no-ask check-in to an existing contact you haven't spoken to in a while. |
| 20 | What's your natural cadence for staying in touch — too rare, too transactional, or about right? |
| 10 | Reflection. |

### Day 7 — What You Want to Be Known For

Reputation is built by being reliably known for something specific — consistency, expertise, generosity — which is what actually makes people want to introduce you to others.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Warm-up. |
| 25 | Write what you'd want to be known for professionally. Compare honestly to your recent actual actions. |
| 20 | What's one concrete action this month that would build the reputation you actually want? |
| 10 | Reflection. |

### Day 8 — Checking Yourself for Transactional Drift

If you're only reaching out to extract value, that's worth noticing and correcting before it becomes the pattern.

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 5 | Warm-up. |
| 25 | Review your last 5 outreach messages. How many offered something vs. were purely extractive? |
| 20 | Pick one relationship to deliberately rebalance this week. |
| 10 | Reflection. |

### Day 9 — Exit Check

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 10 | Plan today's real networking action — a new contact or a meaningful re-engagement. |
| 35 | Do it: self-introduction, value offered or planned, a specific follow-up. |
| 15 | Compare to Day 1's habits — what's different in your approach now? |

**Mastery bar:** you follow up specifically and offer value before asking, as a default, not an exception. If not yet, repeat value-first and follow-up drills with fresh real contacts.
