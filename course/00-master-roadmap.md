# Master Roadmap — The Gaming + Reaction Creator Course

## Who this course is for

You have already decided your niche: **Gaming + Reaction/Commentary**, combined under one personality-driven brand. This course does not spend time on niche selection. It spends 100% of its time teaching you how to make that combination work, how to be the reason people watch, and how to run the whole operation — creative, technical (non-editing), and business — as a beginner with an editor already in place.

## What this course does NOT teach

- Video editing software (Premiere, DaVinci, After Effects, Photoshop). You have an editor. Module 13 teaches you how to *direct* that editor, not replace them.
- Niche selection. Decided.
- Guaranteed virality. Nobody can promise that, and anyone who does is lying to you. This course teaches you how to raise your probability of success and compound it over time.

## How the 24 modules connect

The course moves through four phases. Modules build on each other — later modules assume you've internalized earlier ones.

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Modules 1–3): understand the game before you play it.**
You learn how YouTube actually distributes videos, what a personal brand is and how to build yours, and — critically — how Gaming and Reaction fit under one identity instead of feeling like two random channels mashed together. Nothing you build in later modules will make sense without this.

**Phase 2 — Content Craft (Modules 4–12): make the actual videos good.**
This is the largest phase because it's the highest-leverage skill set: generating ideas, making gaming footage entertaining, making reactions entertaining, titles, thumbnail concepts, scripts, how to talk on camera without dying inside, retention mechanics, and your physical on-camera setup and presence.

**Phase 3 — Production & Distribution (Modules 13–17): get videos made and seen.**
Working with your editor, Shorts, cross-platform promotion, community-building, and reading analytics to close the feedback loop.

**Phase 4 — Business & Systems (Modules 18–24): turn this into a career.**
Growth strategy by subscriber stage, monetization, brand deals, and then three integration modules that turn everything you learned into a concrete 100-video roadmap, a 90-day plan, a weekly production system, and a personal playbook you'll actually use every week for years.

## How each module is structured

1. **Concept** — explained simply, terminology defined the first time it's used.
2. **Why it matters** — the actual mechanism, not a platitude.
3. **Gaming + Reaction application** — how this specifically plays out in your niche.
4. **Examples** — concrete, from creators and formats in your space.
5. **Exercises** — something you do, not just read.
6. **Templates** — copy-paste starting points.
7. **Checklists** — pre-flight checks before you publish.
8. **Common mistakes** — specific failure modes, not generic warnings.
9. **What beginners waste time on** — so you can skip it.
10. **Homework** — the concrete deliverable before moving on.
11. **Checkpoint** — a pass/fail gate. If you can't clear it, redo the module before advancing.

## How to move through the course

- Go in order the first time through. Modules 1–3 recalibrate how you think about everything after them.
- Don't binge-read five modules in a sitting and call it done. Do the exercise for each module before opening the next one. This course is designed to be *used*, not consumed.
- Report your homework results back and ask for a checkpoint review before advancing — that's where the actual coaching happens.
- Revisit Modules 6–11 constantly. They're not "learn once" — they're the muscle you use on every single video.
- Module 24 (Your Personal Playbook) is a living document. You'll draft it early and keep updating it as you learn what actually works for your channel.

## Priority tiers (if you're short on time and need to sequence fast)

- **Tier 1 (do first, non-negotiable):** Modules 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 10 — you cannot make a decent video without these.
- **Tier 2 (do before your first upload):** Modules 4 or 5 (whichever your first video is), 7, 8, 11, 12, 13.
- **Tier 3 (do within your first month):** Modules 14, 15, 16, 17, 23.
- **Tier 4 (do once you have some data/traction):** Modules 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 24.

## Realistic expectations

- Nobody grows in a straight line. You will post videos that get 40 views and videos that outperform your average 10x for reasons you don't fully understand. That's normal, not failure.
- The skills in this course compound. Your 5th video will not look like your 50th. The goal of the first 100 videos (Module 21) is reps, not perfection.
- This course raises your odds. It does not remove randomness from a platform run by an algorithm nobody outside YouTube fully sees.
