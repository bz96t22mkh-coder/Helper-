# Section 20 — Final Mastery

**Length:** 5 days. **No new skills.** This is where you prove it with real evidence, and leave with a plan for keeping it sharp.

---

### Day 1 — Plan the Capstones

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 15 | Reread your Section 19 baseline ratings and decay check. |
| 30 | For each cluster below, pick a real situation from your actual life this week — not a hypothetical. |
| 15 | Which cluster are you most and least confident about going in? |

- **Cluster A — Voice & Connection:** Meta-Learning, English & Articulation, Communication, EQ, Social Skills, Confidence & Charisma, Storytelling.
- **Cluster B — Influence & Leadership:** Critical Thinking, Debating, Negotiation, Networking, Leadership.
- **Cluster C — Mind & Systems:** Productivity, Problem-Solving, Decision-Making, Strategic Thinking, Financial Literacy, Meditation.

### Day 2 — Capstone: Voice & Connection

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 10 | Confirm today's real scenario — a new person, a story to tell, an opinion to defend, an emotionally aware moment. |
| 35 | Execute it for real. |
| 15 | Rate yourself against Sections 1, 3–8, with specific evidence from today. |

### Day 3 — Capstone: Influence & Leadership

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 10 | Confirm today's real scenario — a disagreement, negotiation, networking opportunity, or leadership moment. |
| 35 | Execute it for real. |
| 15 | Rate yourself against Sections 9, 10, 13, 14, 15, with specific evidence. |

### Day 4 — Capstone: Mind & Systems

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 10 | Confirm today's real scenario — a real, moderately complex problem or decision. |
| 35 | Run the full process: root cause, expected value, bias-check, second-order reasoning. Plus a 10–15 min sit and a process-visualization for a real goal. |
| 15 | Rate yourself against Sections 2, 11, 12, 16, 17, 18, with specific evidence. |

### Day 5 — Final Reckoning

| Min | Do |
|---|---|
| 15 | Build the full reassessment table: all 19 skill areas, with evidence from Days 2–4. |
| 25 | For every skill below Highly Proficient, define a specific ongoing maintenance cadence — a weekly drill, a monthly check-in, whatever fits. For skills already strong, define the minimum touch to keep them from decaying. |
| 20 | Write where you started (your first Section 1 baseline), where you are now, and what happens next. |

**Course complete.** From here it's self-directed: run your maintenance plan, and come back to any section for a fresh multi-day refresher whenever real life demands it. Update `course/progress-tracker.md` with your final levels and maintenance cadence.
