## Why this document matters long-term

Without it, every decision (a new video idea, a brand deal offer, a format pivot) gets re-litigated from scratch, which is slow and inconsistent. With it, most decisions become fast checks against a known system, and you reserve real deliberation for genuinely new situations — exactly the same principle from Module 13 applied to your entire operation, not just editing.

## Common mistakes

- Writing the playbook once and never updating it as real data comes in (Module 17, Module 21).
- Treating it as a static "brand bible" instead of a living document that should evolve as you learn what's actually working.
