# /business — your actual company, not course material

Everything in this folder is the real business, not a lesson. As the course (in `/outsourcing-course`) tells you to make a decision, run a calculation, or build a system, the result lands here — decisions log, prospect list, pricing tools, supplier records, customer records, the website, the CRM, marketing material, finance tracking.

- `decisions-log.md` — every real decision made (name, niche, pricing, suppliers), dated, with the reasoning.
- `prospect-list.md` — your real target-customer list, built by you.
- `pricing/`, `suppliers/`, `customers/`, `marketing/`, `finance/`, `website/`, `crm/` — filled in as each module builds them.

Nothing in here is fabricated on your behalf. Anywhere the course needs real-world information only you have (your city, real local businesses, your actual supplier quotes), you provide it and I record/build around it — I don't invent placeholder company names or contacts pretending to be real.
