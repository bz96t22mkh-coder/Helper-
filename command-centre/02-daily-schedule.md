# Daily Schedule + Sustainability Analysis

## 1. Is the current allocation sustainable?

Your stated active-work allocation:

| Block | Hours (focused, excl. breaks) |
|---|---|
| ⭐ Trading | 5.0 |
| ⭐ AI Automation Agency | 2.0 |
| ⭐ Uniform Outsourcing Agency | 2.0 |
| 📚 Reading | 1.0 |
| **Total pure focus time** | **10.0** |

Add the things your brief also requires daily: gym (~50 min), personal development (rotating, 30–60 min), morning/evening routine (~45 min), journaling/day-plan review (~15 min), YouTube (variable, averages ~30–45 min/day once you count both heavy recording days and light days), meals (~90 min total), and micro-breaks inside the 10 hours of focus work (~60–90 min, since 5 straight hours of trading study with zero breaks is not realistic or effective).

**Total wall-clock time from wake to "day's work done": roughly 15.5–16.5 hours.** Waking at 5:00 AM puts "done for the day" at approximately 8:30–9:30 PM, leaving **7.5–8 hours for sleep** if you're asleep by 9:30–10:00 PM.

**Verdict: sustainable, but tight — and only if two things hold:**
1. **Sleep is protected as non-negotiable**, not the variable that absorbs overruns. If a day runs long, the thing that gets cut is a break or reading, never sleep.
2. **YouTube does not get a fixed daily hour** — it flexes around the actual requirement (2 long-form videos/week), which is why it's built as a variable block below, not a 6th fixed hour. Forcing a daily YouTube hour on top of the existing 10 would push wake-to-sleep past 17 hours, which is not sustainable alongside 5 AM wake-ups.

This matches your own brief (section 20/31): don't force every activity into every day if it's inefficient. The structure below reflects that.

## 2. The core rule (do not violate this)

**Stable phase → this schedule stays exactly as written, every day, for weeks/months.** Only the *content* inside each block changes day to day (which lesson, which trade setups, which video stage). The schedule itself only changes when a Phase's "Complete Phase" button is clicked (see `01-notion-architecture.md` §9) and the new phase's allocation genuinely differs.

## 4. YOUTUBE — variable block, not a fixed daily hour

Target: 2 long-form videos/week. Workflow: Idea → Research → Record → Send to Editor → Editing (editor's job, off your clock) → Thumbnail → Review → Revisions → Approval → Upload → Promotion → Analytics.

| Day type | What you actually do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Recording day** (pick 1–2 fixed days/week) | Idea finalize + record (footage/reaction) for both week's videos back-to-back | 60–90 min |
| **Editor-handoff / review day** | Send footage, review previous video's edit, approve/request revisions, pick thumbnail | 15–20 min |
| **Upload day** (2x/week) | Final check, upload, write description/tags, post to X/Reddit where relevant | 20–30 min |
| **Repurposing day** (day after each upload) | Clip 2–3 Shorts/TikTok/Reels from the long-form video, schedule them | 20–30 min |
| **Quiet days** | Just check pipeline status in the Today dashboard | 5 min |

Average across a week: roughly 3–3.5 hours/week, or ~30–40 min/day amortized — which is why it's the flexible slot in the schedule above rather than a fixed hour that's wasted on quiet days.

## 5. SUNDAY / LIGHT DAY

Keep gym, but drop one business block to a lighter maintenance check-in and use the freed time for the **Weekly Review** (see `05-finance-habits-reviews.md`) and Habit Tracker/Time Summary review. Suggested: Trading and both agencies drop to 1–2 hours of review/planning (not full focused blocks), YouTube gets its recording session if it's a recording day, and you get 2–3 hours back for rest, life admin, or catching up on any carried-over course content. This is the day the Weekly Time Summary (Planned vs Actual) actually gets read.

## 6. WHAT TRIGGERS A SCHEDULE CHANGE (and what doesn't)

Reference table — also lives in `03-goals-and-phases.md`, repeated here because it's the crux of your entire brief:

| Does NOT change the schedule | DOES change the schedule |
|---|---|
| A new day starting | A Phase's "Complete Phase" button is clicked |
| Finishing one lesson/module | A course is fully completed |
| A slightly slow or fast day | A pillar moves from learning → execution (e.g. Trading: course → backtesting) |
| Being 15–20 min over/under on a block | A major milestone/goal is confirmed reached |
| Normal daily variation in energy | A future goal unlocks (e.g. Crypto) |
| | Workload materially changes (e.g. first paying client changes AI Agency from 2h learning to 2h+ delivery work) |
